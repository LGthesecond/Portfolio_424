Lorcan Grace's Portfolio

Beat Punk Mania

Beat Punk Mania was a project I helped to develop during 3rd year of Computer Game Applications Development at Abertay. It is a 1v1 rhythm game where both players create a chart (a series of notes on the beat) for the other player to execute. I worked largely on the opening character select screen and the abilities (which unfortunately didn’t make it into the final game). This is the biggest project I have worked on and am immensely proud of the work which was produced, as well as how it honed my creative and programming skills. Click to see Itch page!

Ailrus Haunted House

In 2nd year at Abertay I worked on another game, a 2.5D sidescroller utilising propulsion based movement inspired by Angry Birds. It was also built exclusively for PS5 consoles! I was responsible for creating the 2D assets the game uses, along with game design and helped conceptualise and implement the prupulsion feature itself.
